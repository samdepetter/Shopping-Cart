# Shopping-Cart

Boodschappen & gerechten — shopping list, pantry estimate and recipes for Colruyt Collect&Go.

Live: https://samdepetter.github.io/Shopping-Cart/

- `index.html` — the app (no build step)
- `data/` — product catalog, purchase history and recipes
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `icons/` — lets it open full screen from the phone's home screen

Your cart, plan and corrections are saved on the device (browser storage), not in the repo.
